About

Hello, I’m Jules

After 25 years as an account manager in the UK ski and outdoor industry, redundancy gave me the opportunity to step away from corporate life and turn a long-standing passion into a career.

Having spent years painting and decorating for friends and family, I decided it was time to make it official – and so, The Paintress was born.

I have a natural eye for interiors and colour, with experience ranging from complete renovations to refreshing and revitalising individual spaces. I love creating homes that feel personal, considered and connected to the people who live in them.

From choosing colours that complement treasured furniture and accessories to drawing inspiration from nature and the surrounding environment, I bring a thoughtful and creative approach to every project.

Take a look through my gallery to see some of my work, and explore below to see how I can help transform your space.
